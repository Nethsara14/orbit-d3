Legacy

The Lunar Roving Vehicle let astronauts explore much farther from their lander than ever before. On Apollo 15 it carried Scott and Irwin to mountains, craters and the edge of a deep canyon, and brought back rocks that helped scientists understand how the Moon formed.

When the mission ended, the rover was left where Scott parked it, near Hadley Rille. Rovers were too heavy to bring home, so each of the three Moon buggies stayed behind. They are part of the equipment humans have left on the Moon, still sitting where their drivers last stopped them.

All facts on this page are adapted from official NASA Apollo 15 mission records .
