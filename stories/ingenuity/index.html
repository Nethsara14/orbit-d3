Legacy

Ingenuity proved that powered, controlled flight is possible in the very thin air of Mars. What started as a small technology demonstration became an aerial scout that flew 72 times, far beyond its original plan of 5 flights.

Today, Ingenuity rests silently at Valinor Hills in Jezero Crater, where its final flight ended in January 2024. Its success opened the door to future aircraft on Mars and other worlds, working alongside rovers such as Perseverance to explore places that wheels cannot easily reach.
